## Shroudly 2.6.61

**Reports now carry what it takes to find a problem at once, the same on every device: every setting as it acts, what Shroudly did to each connection, and what runs beside it. The lite Android APK says when a site or app stays blocked, and offers the full APK, whose Tor can open it, installed in one tap.** Also sent to the App Store and Google Play.

### Android, the lite APK: a site or app that stays blocked
- The lite APK (`-Android-lite.apk`) has no Tor. A site no technique opened, or whose addresses the network does not let through at all (Instagram, WhatsApp and Telegram in Iran, for one), stayed shut without a word, while the full APK would have asked to open it through Tor. Now the same moment brings a card on the main screen: the site or app, why it stays shut, and **Get the full version**. That downloads the full APK of the same version (from GitHub, or Shroudly's download server where GitHub is blocked), checks that it is Shroudly signed with the same certificate, and hands it to Android's installer: it installs over the lite one and keeps every setting. **Not now** rests for a week. A notification (a message on the screen of a TV) says it at most once a day.

### Reports, the same on every device
A report from *Something not working?* now says at once what is going on, the same on computers, Android phones, tablets and TVs, iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV:
- **Every setting as it acts**, those never changed too (one left at its default was not in the report before, so it could not say, say, whether Tor was on), and which were changed from the defaults. Your own server or DNS only as set or not, your lists only as how many entries.
- **The connections this session**: the names Shroudly did something to (a technique, Tor, your own server) and how, and the connections of common apps (Discord, YouTube, Instagram, WhatsApp, Telegram, Roblox, Riot's games and the like) and whether Shroudly touched them at all. Other sites that went through untouched are left out: they are your browsing.
- **What runs beside the engine and what Shroudly found**: Advanced mode and why not, Discord's and the games' relays, Tor and how far it got, the services the autopilot found blocked or open on this network, other VPNs or proxies in the way (computers); the network's own state (all).
- **Android:** which APK it is (full, arm64, lite) and where Tor stands.
- **Computers:** a Start or Stop pressed by the user is a line of the log, so a stop and a start half a second apart are no longer a riddle.
- The [privacy policy](https://codeshare.me/projects/shroudly/privacy) says it in full.

### Logs: longer, without the noise
- **3000 lines** on every device (were 300 on Android and the Apple app, 500 on computers), so a report reaches back days, not minutes.
- **Another app trying servers** (a VPN or proxy app going down a list of addresses through Shroudly) wrote a line for each, twenty a minute: now three a minute, and the rest in one line with how many.
- **Android:** the routine check that the way through works, every two minutes, wrote "all fine" each time; now once after a start, when it was slow, and once in half an hour. A check for any other reason is always written.

### What's new, once
- Computers: a version that changes nothing there does not show the notes before it again.
