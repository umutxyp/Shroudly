## Shroudly 2.4.7

- **Roblox works as an app, not only in the browser.** The Roblox app and its installer ignore the system proxy, so on filtered networks the installer stopped at *Continue installation without flag settings from clientsettingscdn.roblox.com* and the app could not reach Roblox, while roblox.com opened fine in the browser. The new **Games and apps** mode (Settings, or the offer Shroudly shows when Roblox is installed) sends them through Shroudly, together with other games, launchers and tools that ignore the system proxy. Windows and macOS, no admin rights. If Roblox is already open, Shroudly offers to restart it; on Windows, if you start Roblox from your browser, close the browser completely and open it again once. A proxy you set yourself is never replaced, and everything is put back when you turn the mode off or quit Shroudly — also after a crash or a power cut. [How it works](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly#games-and-apps-mode-roblox)
- **The site list keeps itself current.** For people who route only listed sites, the list grows from about 100 to over 3,000 domains. It is rebuilt every week from [OONI](https://ooni.org)'s measurements in more than 30 countries, Turkey first. Newly blocked sites are added, and sites that have been unblocked everywhere for 90 days are dropped. Each service comes with every domain it needs to load properly, its CDN and sign-in captcha included. The app picks the new list up by itself about once a day, through the protected connection. A list you have edited stays yours. [How it works](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly#the-automatic-site-list)
- **Lighter in the background.** The helper that puts your proxy settings back if Shroudly is ever killed no longer wakes up every second. It now sleeps until Shroudly ends, then restores everything in about a third of a second.
- **Discord app mode works with Discord Development too.** Only Stable, PTB and Canary were covered, so the Development build stayed on *Checking for updates* ([#6](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/issues/6)). After the update, Shroudly offers to restart Discord Development so it picks the change up.

Everything from [2.4.6](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/releases/tag/v2.4.6) is included. Users of 2.0.4 and later update automatically.
