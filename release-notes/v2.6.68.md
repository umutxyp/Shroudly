## Shroudly 2.6.68

**No more "Roblox does not work on this network" in the middle of a game on Turkish networks, reports that get through from Russia, on top of 2.6.67's fixes.**

### Computers (Windows, macOS, Linux)
- On some Turkish networks one of Roblox's addresses (silver.roblox.com) is blocked by its address while every game server and the rest of Roblox open through Shroudly. That one address was told as all of Roblox blocked ("only a VPN gets you there"), with a notification during play. Now the warning comes only when Roblox's other addresses are not working either.

### Reports (computers, Android, the Mac App Store app)
- A report that cannot reach the developer the usual way goes once more through Tor while Tor is ready. In Russia, networks slow down the addresses of Cloudflare, where Shroudly's report server is, until nothing gets through (other VPNs there with them), and reports could not be sent at all.

### Also in 2.6.67 (if you are coming from an earlier version)
- Roblox's maps, items and pictures load again where they had started to go missing after a while; the engine no longer drops techniques that work when an app closes a connection.
- When Tor carried one name of a service, the service's other domains no longer go to Tor after a few seconds.
- A name that does not exist no longer starts Tor; Android no longer turns protection off while connections are flowing.
- With your own server, WebRTC no longer shows the device's own address (Android, iPhone, iPad, Mac App Store app).
