## Shroudly 2.6.12

**On Android, protection now stays on until you turn it off, even on phones that end apps while the screen is locked.**

### Android: back by itself ([#39](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/issues/39))
- Some phones end apps while the screen is locked, often with a power saver that also turns Wi-Fi and mobile data off. Protection then ended with the app, and after unlocking you had to open Shroudly and start it again.
- Now Shroudly comes back by itself as soon as a network is there again, without a tap. Android keeps a small job for it that waits for the network, so this works even when the phone has ended the app.
- The same happens when protection ended on its own, for example because the tunnel kept failing. It waits two minutes first and tries at most three times an hour, so a network where it cannot work is not retried over and over.
- If you turned protection off yourself, or another VPN app took over, it stays off.
- While there is no network at all, Shroudly no longer checks the connection every few seconds. Those checks could only fail, and they kept a locked phone awake. The first check comes as soon as the network is back.
- If Android does not let the app start in the background (Proxy only on some phones), a notice brings protection back with one tap.
- Android's own **Always-on VPN** does the same from the system's settings and also covers phones that stop apps completely. Turn it on in Android's VPN settings for the strongest guarantee.

Everything from [2.6.11](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/releases/tag/v2.6.11) is included: Advanced mode now answers the DNS of programs that ignore the proxy with encrypted DNS. Desktop users update automatically; the Android app tells you that 2.6.12 is out.
