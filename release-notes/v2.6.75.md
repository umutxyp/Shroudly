## Shroudly 2.6.75

**Tor only when it is the answer; every technique on the first visit; http:// sites that the network drops without a word; apps that keep addresses after a network change; logos to choose from.**

### Desktop, Android and the Mac
- "It worked in the old version; since the update it is slow." We measured the engines of 2.6.50 to 2.6.74 on a network that drops blocked handshakes without a word (Türk Telekom, Turkcell). One connection had time for four techniques. Before Tor, the next visits carried on down the list until one opened the site; since 2.6.54 the first visit that ran out of time handed the site to Tor, and nothing looked further, so sites that a later technique opens lived on slow Tor. Now the rest of the list goes out at once after two unanswered attempts: every technique on the first visit, in about the same time.
- Tor takes a site only when every technique failed, when no address of it answers, or when you chose it. A site on Tor is looked at again behind it and goes back to its own way as soon as that works, instead of staying on Tor for a quarter of an hour.
- Tor is no longer asked for or started because the network went away for a moment (a name must stay out of reach for ten seconds first), for Shroudly's own encrypted DNS, or (desktop) while another bypass tool such as GoodbyeDPI is running.
- A site's other names, while Tor carries one of them, get every technique too, quickly, instead of six seconds and then Tor (Roblox froze and cut out on Türk Telekom this way).
- A request nobody answered counts for half of a real cut on the way to Tor.

### Desktop, Android and the Apple apps
- http:// sites that the network drops without a word (Türk Telekom) opened neither directly nor through Tor, and nothing was said. Shroudly now tells a dropped request from a slow server (the request was never acknowledged) and tries its techniques, then Tor where it may.
- Logos to choose from in Settings: styles, special days and the countries where people use Shroudly against blocking. Inside the app everywhere; also the home-screen icon on Android, iPhone and iPad, the Dock on the Mac, and the window and taskbar on Windows and Linux.

### Android, iPhone and iPad
- TikTok froze after the phone switched between Wi-Fi and mobile data: it kept the addresses the old tunnel had handed out and went on asking for them. Such a connection is now taken by the name in what the app sends first.

### Desktop
- Games and apps mode: programs built on older versions of axios (the TruckersMP launcher: "Request failed with status code 405") send a whole https:// address instead of asking for a tunnel; Shroudly now makes the request for them, with the certificate checked.
- The report no longer lists Shroudly's own Tor as "Also running: Tor". Tor's question names the app (X for its image servers, NVIDIA's app). NVIDIA's app is in "Apps always through Tor".
- A connection frozen at about the same amount again and again (Iran, Shatel: X's images) goes to Tor after three times.

### Android
- Changing the mode in Settings soon after a stop no longer ends the app ("Stopping took more than 15 s").
