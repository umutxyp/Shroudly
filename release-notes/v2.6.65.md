## Shroudly 2.6.65

**QUIC off by default on phones, tablets and the Mac App Store app, so apps use the connections Shroudly can work on; reports that show datagrams and Discord's own last words.**

### QUIC off by default (Android, iPhone, iPad, Mac App Store app)
- Over QUIC (the UDP way YouTube, Instagram and Google use first) no technique works, and censors slow it down: a Turkish report had YouTube thumbnails crawling. With QUIC off, apps go over TCP, where Shroudly does its work; what QUIC saves is one round trip when a connection opens (tens of milliseconds), not download speed. **Settings → Connection → Advanced → Turn off QUIC** turns it back on. A choice you made yourself is kept. On computers Advanced mode already did this.

### Reports and the Connections list
- Datagrams (QUIC, games, calls) now show in **Connections by site** and in reports, as **UDP**, with what went each way: an app that is slow over UDP shows where it is. Computers, Android and Apple devices.
- A site you block in the Connections list has its datagrams blocked too, not only its connections.
- Computers: a report carries the last lines of Discord's own log (the newest of the last day, your user name taken out) and whether its updater goes through Shroudly, so a Discord stuck on its **Starting** screen says why.
