## Shroudly 2.4.8

- **Glass looks like one light again.** With glass on, the top of the window was lit by two separate glows, one in each corner, with a darker gap between them. It is now a single light falling from the top centre, matching the one rising from the bottom. The window-button strip at the very top, and the tab bar with the "A Codeshare product" line at the bottom, are now plain and share one colour, so the light belongs to the content between them. On Windows and Linux the area behind the caption buttons takes the same colour.
- **The "A Codeshare product" line sits in its own strip.** It was pressed right up against the tab labels above it. It now has a hairline above it and sits centred in its strip, with glass on or off.

Everything from [2.4.7](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/releases/tag/v2.4.7) is included. Users of 2.0.4 and later update automatically.
