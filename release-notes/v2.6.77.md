## Shroudly 2.6.77

**Shroudly is on Google Play. Store copies update as soon as a new version is out; other copies are offered the store, never pushed.**

### Android
- The copy from Google Play asks for each new version with Play's full-screen update as soon as it is out, each time the window opens. Protection stays on while it waits: on a censored network the update itself may need Shroudly.
- The APK from GitHub offers the Google Play copy (not where Play does not work); it keeps updating itself from GitHub as before.

### iPhone, iPad and the Mac
- The App Store copy asks to update as soon as the App Store has a newer version, with a button to the App Store; protection stays on meanwhile. An .ipa installed another way is offered the App Store, never pushed. Both start when the App Store listing is live.

### Desktop
- Nothing changed in the app; the README, the wiki and codeshare.me link Shroudly's Google Play listing.
