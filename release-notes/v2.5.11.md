## Shroudly 2.5.11

- **Security updates.** The tool that builds Shroudly's window (Next.js) is updated to version 16, which also replaces an older copy of PostCSS it carried. The app looks and works exactly as in 2.5.10; every package was installed and started on its own system before it was published.
- **Safer process checks.** The names Shroudly looks for in the list of running programs (Discord, Roblox and Roblox Studio, their installers) are now matched exactly, whatever characters they contain.

Everything from [2.5.10](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/releases/tag/v2.5.10) is included: the autopilot that turns on what is needed and restarts what is stuck by itself, Roblox Studio through Shroudly, and the Windows ARM64 installer fix. Users of 2.0.4 and later update automatically.
