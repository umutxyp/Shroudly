## Shroudly 2.6.0 — now on Android

**Shroudly for Android is here.** Download `Shroudly-2.6.0-Android.apk` on your phone, open it, tap the big button and allow the VPN request. Every app on the phone then goes through Shroudly: browsers, Discord, Roblox and other games, with no extra modes to turn on.

- **The same engine as the desktop**, with the same automatic techniques, remembered techniques and encrypted DNS. Nothing goes through a server of ours: the "VPN" is local and only reshapes the phone's own connections.
- **Games and voice work too**: UDP traffic (game servers, voice calls) is carried, and apps' own DNS lookups are encrypted.
- **Quick Settings tile** to turn it on and off, and Android's Always-on VPN brings it back after a restart.
- **Holds up on its own**: if something stops it (another VPN, the system), it tells you; the engine restarts by itself if it ever stops responding.
- **31 languages**, following your phone's language. Android 8 or newer, every common phone (ARM 64/32-bit and x86_64), about 1 MB.
- The app tells you when a newer version is out; the APK is signed with the project's own key (certificate SHA-256 `23:AC:01:4B:72:26:34:3B:74:C7:C9:C0:A4:FD:6F:1D:ED:97:51:8A:3B:FD:35:BC:DE:65:B6:C1:EC:A8:BD:76`).

Android asks once to allow installs from your browser, since the app is not on Google Play yet. Advanced mode is not available on Android (it would need root), and Android allows one VPN at a time.

**For the desktop**, the engine underneath also got safer:
- Names on your local network (printers, routers, `.lan` names) resolve while protection is on, and a public name can never be pointed at a device on your network.
- Advanced mode stops sending its decoy to a server the decoy reached and upset (a server only a few hops away), and keeps only the safe part of the technique there, so such sites open on the next try.

Everything from [2.5.14](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/releases/tag/v2.5.14) is included. Desktop users of 2.0.4 and later update automatically.
