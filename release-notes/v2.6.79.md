## Shroudly 2.6.79

**Roblox games join again in Turkey; casting to a TV works with protection on (Android); an app blocked by its servers' addresses gets the Tor question; reports must come from a real try.**

### Desktop
- Roblox: joining a game failed with an HTTP error on Türk Telekom. Its DNS gives Roblox the block page over IPv4 and its own server over IPv6; when the addresses from encrypted DNS did not answer, Shroudly tried the network's and "opened" Roblox there. A name the network's DNS answers with a block page is now never followed there.
- A report goes only when the log shows Shroudly was started, and the site or app field must name one (not "VPN", "internet" or "everything").

### Android
- The local network and multicast stay off the VPN: casting to a TV, finding devices and a TV running Shroudly answering its network all work with protection on.
- An app no list knows that cannot reach three of its servers within a minute gets the Tor question (as a program does on the desktop).
- The Roblox fix above.
- "Something not working?" shows in Iran too, with the same stricter report rules.

### iPhone, iPad and the Mac
- The stricter report rules; the Mac also has the Roblox fix.
