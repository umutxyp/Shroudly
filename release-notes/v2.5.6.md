## Shroudly 2.5.6

- **Advanced mode: the TCP techniques GoodbyeDPI and zapret use.** On networks that need Advanced mode, the packet-layer engine now does more than send the decoy packet. It **drops the forged resets** some providers inject to tear a blocked connection down, so the real connection stays up; a genuine reset from the site is never touched. It **measures how far away the server is** and sets the decoy to expire just before reaching it, instead of guessing a fixed distance. It can also **overlap** the first segment, or split it with a fake at the seam (`split-seqovl`, `fakedsplit`, `fakeddisorder`), the way zapret does. The first two are on by default because they can only help; the others are switches, and the proven decoy stays the default. They work alongside 2.5.5's QUIC-to-TCP and Discord voice decoys. Windows and Linux; Advanced mode still asks for administrator permission and undoes everything when you stop it.
- **Games and apps mode: the Roblox buttons sit under their switch.** **Open Roblox** and **Install Roblox** were pushed against the divider line below the switch; they are now part of the same row, with room above them.

Everything from [2.5.5](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/releases/tag/v2.5.5) is included. Users of 2.0.4 and later update automatically.
