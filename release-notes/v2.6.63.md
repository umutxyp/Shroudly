## Shroudly 2.6.63

**A Mac App Store app as light as the GitHub one, and from the latest reports: Tor asked about again instead of turned off for good, no stray Tor after a Stop, and reports that say enough the first time.** On GitHub only for now; the App Store and Google Play have 2.6.61 in review, and their testers get this version.

### Mac App Store app: as light as the Mac app from GitHub
- The Mac App Store app sent every connection of the Mac through its tunnel, so the whole Mac ran slower than with the app from GitHub. It now works the way the GitHub app does: Shroudly is the Mac's proxy, browsers and the apps that use the system's proxy go through it, and the rest go straight. For apps that ignore the proxy (the Discord app, games), **Settings → All apps through Shroudly (VPN)** puts every app through it as before. iPhone, iPad and Apple TV are unchanged.

### The last way out (Tor): asked again, not dropped for good
- Saying **No** to Tor once (or turning it off in Settings) turned it off for good, and afterwards a site that only Tor could open on that network (X and YouTube in Iran, for one) failed with "Shroudly keeps trying other ways", with no word that Tor was the one way left. Now, when a site stays closed after every technique, Shroudly asks again with the same card, at most once a week: **Use Tor** turns it back on, **No** waits another week. It asks only when Advanced mode is already on (or cannot run there), never in the Android APK without Tor, and never with your own server. Computers, Android and the Mac.

### The last way out: no stray Tor after Stop
- A connection that ended just as protection stopped could start Tor once more with nothing to stop it, and on Android the next start's Tor then ended at once ("Tor ended (1)") again and again. A stopping engine no longer starts Tor. Computers and Android.

### Reports
- A report's list of connections waited a fixed moment for the engine's answer and could come out empty on a busy phone. It now waits for the answer itself (at most 3 seconds). Computers and Android.
- Each connection in a report now says how many are still open, how much went each way and when the name was last used, so a connection that got nothing back shows as such. Computers, Android and Apple devices.
- The log now names the site that made Tor start ("starting Tor" alone could not say whether it was the app the report was about). Computers and Android.
- A report also carries the engine's last 80 troubles with sites (blocked, cut, unreachable, the last way out), which the log says only once per site, and on computers whether each Discord app runs and reaches Shroudly, and what its updater relay served. Fewer reports need a second one.
