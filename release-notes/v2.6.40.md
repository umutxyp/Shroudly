## Shroudly 2.6.40

**Faster, with fewer pages, games and apps that now and then do not open.** Thanks to everyone who sent their logs.

### Encrypted DNS: sites you use load without waiting for a lookup
- Shroudly keeps the addresses of the sites you use, so it does not have to look them up again. It had room for only 256 answers, fewer than a few busy pages with their ads and images, or the apps on a phone, use within minutes. Names you were using were pushed out, and new connections waited for an encrypted DNS lookup again, sometimes for seconds on a slow network. Now it keeps 4096, sixteen times as many.
- When a site's address is due to be checked again, the one Shroudly has is used at once while a fresh one is fetched in the background. Before, the connection waited for the new lookup.
- **Android:** the encrypted DNS answers are now also kept for apps that ask DNS themselves; before, every one of their lookups went out over the network again.

### Android: room for many more games, calls and apps at once
- On the phone every game, voice call and app that uses QUIC (YouTube, Instagram, Chrome) sends its UDP through Shroudly. The engine took at most 256 of these flows at once, and each stayed open for two minutes after its last packet; even one DNS lookup held a flow for two minutes. On a busy phone the table filled up, and a game that wanted to connect, a call, or a page got no answer at all.
- Now it takes 1024 flows at once, and a flow that only carried a DNS lookup ends after 15 seconds. Should the table ever fill up, the log says so.

### Also
- Where the network has no IPv6, Shroudly no longer tries the IPv6 address the network's own DNS gives for a site that is hard to reach.

Everything from [2.6.39](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/releases/tag/v2.6.39) is included. Desktop users update automatically; the Android app tells you that 2.6.40 is out.
