## Shroudly 2.6.52

**The iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV app catches up with Android, widgets everywhere, and Shroudly itself helps people in censored countries.**

### iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV
- **Live stats** on the main screen: connections now and in all, how long protection has been on, what went down and up, and how many people are online now.
- **Block ads and trackers** (Settings, off by default), with your own exceptions. The blocking is the engine's, as on the computer and Android; the list is a compact one (the AdGuard DNS filter and StevenBlack's hosts, 220,000 names) so the VPN stays well inside the memory iOS gives it.
- **A DNS server of your own** (Settings → DNS server): DNS over HTTPS, DNS over TLS, plain DNS over TCP or UDP, tested when you tap Use, and *Only this server* if you want nothing else asked. Its name is looked up through Shroudly's encrypted DNS; DoH and DoT go through the engine.
- **Accent colour**: the logo's blue or one of nine others, as on Android and the computer.
- **Count me in the live total** (on by default, as on the other apps): the same anonymous request the computer sends, through the protected connection.
- **Widgets** for the Home Screen and Lock Screen (iPhone, iPad) and the Mac desktop, with a switch on iPhone and iPad; on iOS 18, a **Control Center** switch. **Shortcuts and Siri** start and stop protection.
- **About**: the release notes and the licences of what is built in.
- On a wide, low screen (an unfolded iPhone Duo, Apple TV) the switch and its notice sit side by side, so nothing needs scrolling.
- The privacy policy now lives on the company's site: [codeshare.me/projects/shroudly/privacy](https://codeshare.me/projects/shroudly/privacy).

### Help people in censored countries, inside Shroudly
- Until now a phone pointed you to another app for this. Now Shroudly runs a Snowflake proxy for the Tor network itself, the same one as on the computer: on iPhone and iPad while the app is open, on the Mac while it runs, and on Android while protection is on and the phone is charging on Wi-Fi (no battery, no mobile data). Off until you turn it on (About on Apple, Settings on Android). Conduit stays as a way to help all day.

### Android
- A **home-screen widget** that shows whether protection is on and switches it with a tap.

### All platforms
- Block ads and trackers reads its list into a buffer of the list's own size: half the memory at its peak while a new list is put in use.

Everything from [2.6.51](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/releases/tag/v2.6.51) is included. Desktop users update automatically; the Android app from GitHub offers to install 2.6.52 itself.
