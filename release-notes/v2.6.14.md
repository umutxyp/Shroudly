## Shroudly 2.6.14

**A new technique: the TLS record cut one byte before the end of the site's name, the one that opens YouTube's video servers in Iran.**

### YouTube videos in Iran ([#37](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/issues/37))
- On some networks every cut at or near the start of a site's name is dropped, but a cut one byte before the name ends gets through. That is ByeByeDPI's `-r-1+se`, which plays YouTube's videos in Iran where all of Shroudly's 19 techniques failed.
- Shroudly now has this cut too (`tlsrec-sni-end`), tried right after the other record cuts. If a site's first name gets nowhere with those, its next name starts with this one (YouTube's video servers each have a name of their own). Whatever works is remembered for the site, as before.
- Together with 2.6.13 (a site's cache inside the country, from your network's own DNS), Shroudly now covers both ways YouTube's videos are blocked there.

Everything from [2.6.13](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/releases/tag/v2.6.13) is included. Desktop users update automatically; the Android app tells you that 2.6.14 is out.
