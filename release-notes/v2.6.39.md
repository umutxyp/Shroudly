## Shroudly 2.6.39

**Support Shroudly is now one click away on the main screen of the desktop app too.**

- On Windows, macOS and Linux, the Protection screen has a quiet *Support Shroudly* link under the start/stop button, always there, as on the Android app's main screen. It opens About → Support Shroudly: PayPal, or a bank transfer from your own country, with every line ready to copy.
- The occasional card above it works as in 2.6.38: first after three days of use, then at most every two months after *Not now*.
- The [Shroudly page on codeshare.me](https://codeshare.me/projects/shroudly#support) has the same section.

Everything from [2.6.38](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/releases/tag/v2.6.38) is included. Desktop users update automatically; the Android app tells you that 2.6.39 is out.
