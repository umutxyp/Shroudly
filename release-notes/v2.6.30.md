## Shroudly 2.6.30

**Fixes a 2.6.29 regression where Advanced mode could stop almost everything from connecting.**

### Connectivity restored
- 2.6.29 turned on TLS-record splitting by default in Advanced mode. That had an unintended effect: Advanced mode also began reshaping Shroudly's own proxy connections — which the engine had already handled — and broke them, so on some setups nearly nothing would connect.
- This release puts that technique back to off-by-default (exactly as 2.6.28 worked). Everything connects as it did before. The technique is still available for people who turn it on deliberately for programs that bypass the proxy.

Everything from [2.6.28](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/releases/tag/v2.6.28) is included. Desktop users update automatically; the Android app tells you that 2.6.30 is out.
