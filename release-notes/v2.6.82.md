## Shroudly 2.6.82

**The store copies follow the stores' rules on helping others; remote settings named in What's new.**

### Google Play, the App Store and TestFlight
- "Help people in censored countries" inside Shroudly (a Snowflake proxy for the Tor network) is left out of the store copies: Google Play and the App Store take an app that lets other people use the device as a proxy only when that is what the app is for. The link to Psiphon's Conduit, an app made for it, stays. The APK and the IPA from GitHub, and the desktop, keep it as before. Ad blocking on Android was already only in the APK from GitHub.

### Every system
- What's new now says what 2.6.81 brought: Shroudly gets better settings for your network without waiting for an update, signed and checked (remote settings).
