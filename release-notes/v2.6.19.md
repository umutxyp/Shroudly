## Shroudly 2.6.19

**The ad and tracker blocker now blocks what uBlock Origin Lite blocks by name, and more, on the desktop and on Android.**

### Block ads and trackers: a new list
- Until now the blocker used one public hosts list. Measured against the lists uBlock Origin Lite uses (EasyList, EasyPrivacy, uBlock Origin's own, Peter Lowe's), it covered only about 5% of the domains they block.
- It now uses Shroudly's own list, built every week from AdGuard DNS filter, HaGeZi's Multi PRO, EasyList, EasyPrivacy and StevenBlack/hosts. That is about 345 000 names.
  - It covers over 99% of the domains uBlock Origin Lite blocks.
  - It blocks 130 of the 131 names in the d3ward ad block test (the old list: 102).
  - It also blocks the trackers of phones and TVs.
  - It takes 4 MB of memory.
- The lists' own exceptions are now honoured, so a name they take back from a broader rule stays reachable.
- A week whose sources fail, or whose result would block a site everyone needs (Google, YouTube, Discord, Roblox, your bank's login pages), publishes nothing, and the list you have stays.
- Your list is replaced at once after the update, not after a week.
- It blocks by name, as AdGuard DNS or NextDNS do. It cannot hide empty ad boxes inside a page, or block ads served from the site's own name (YouTube's video ads). Only a browser extension can do that.

Everything from [2.6.18](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/releases/tag/v2.6.18) is included. Desktop users update automatically; the Android app tells you that 2.6.19 is out.
