## Shroudly 2.6.33

**Shroudly never takes the screen from a full-screen game again.**

### Nothing pops up over your game
- When a site could not be opened, Shroudly used to bring its own window to the front to suggest Advanced mode, and that threw a full-screen game back to the desktop, sometimes in the middle of a match. The window now never comes up by itself. You get a notification instead (Windows holds notifications back while you play), and clicking it opens Shroudly with the suggestion waiting.
- Things Shroudly does on its own now wait while a full-screen game, video or presentation is in front, and happen once you are out of it:
  - the permission prompt for Advanced mode, when the autopilot asks for it or brings it back;
  - the permission prompt for Microsoft Store apps (Minecraft is one);
  - restarting Discord when it does not get through;
  - restarting to install an update.
- This covers games in "borderless" or "windowed full screen" mode as well as true full screen. It works on Windows, and on Linux under X11.

Everything from [2.6.32](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/releases/tag/v2.6.32) is included. Desktop users update automatically; the Android app tells you that 2.6.33 is out.
