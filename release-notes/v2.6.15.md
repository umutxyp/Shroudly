## Shroudly 2.6.15

**Messages in chat apps on Android no longer go late, a site that needs your network's own DNS opens on the first try, and Auto mode being off is said plainly.**

### Android: chat apps and push notifications
- Chat apps keep a connection open that stays quiet until a new message arrives: Instagram's messages, WhatsApp, Telegram and Discord do, and so does Android's push channel. The tunnel ended any connection that carried nothing for five minutes. Until the app noticed and connected again, messages did not go out, or went late.
- Quiet connections are now kept for up to an hour, which is longer than these apps wait between their checks. A connection whose server is really gone is still closed. Quiet UDP (calls, QUIC) now gets two minutes, not one.

### A site's address from your network's own DNS, on the first try ([#40](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/issues/40))
- Since 2.6.13, when no address from encrypted DNS answers, Shroudly also tries the one your network's DNS gives. That took a reload of the page. Now it happens within the same connection, so the page loads on the first try.

### Auto mode off, said plainly ([#41](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/issues/41))
- With Auto mode off in Settings, every site gets only the one technique chosen there. When the network cut a site, the log still said "Shroudly is trying other techniques", which was not true. Now the log and the notice say that Auto mode is off and which technique is used, and the notice turns Auto mode on with one click.

### Android: the Quick Settings tile, easier to find
- Shroudly has had a tile for the panel at the top of the screen, which turns protection on and off with one tap. Few people found it, because a tile shows only once you add it yourself. **Settings → Quick Settings tile** now adds it: on Android 13 and later with one tap, on older versions with directions.

Everything from [2.6.14](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/releases/tag/v2.6.14) is included. Desktop users update automatically; the Android app tells you that 2.6.15 is out.
