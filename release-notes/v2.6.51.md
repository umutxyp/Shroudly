## Shroudly 2.6.51

**Apple TV, a Mac window of the right size, and the App Store.**

### Apple TV
- Shroudly now runs on Apple TV (tvOS 17 or newer), the same app and packet tunnel as on iPhone and iPad. It is used with the remote: the round switch gets a ring in the accent colour when it is reached.
- A layered Apple TV icon and a Top Shelf image. Web addresses (source code, privacy policy, guides) are shown as text, since Apple TV has no browser.

### Mac
- The Mac app opened as a window as tall as the screen. It now opens at 480 by 680, the width of the desktop app's window, and its wide scroll bars are gone.

### iPhone and iPad
- On a small iPhone (SE) the shutdown notice's title was cut off; it now wraps, and the main screen scrolls when a notice and a long language do not fit.

### App Store
- iPhone, iPad and Mac have been sent to the App Store, in 22 store languages; Apple TV follows with this version.

Everything from [2.6.50](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/releases/tag/v2.6.50) is included: Android TV and Google TV, and a site whose server answers with the wrong certificate opens again. The computer and Android apps are unchanged from 2.6.50 apart from the version.
