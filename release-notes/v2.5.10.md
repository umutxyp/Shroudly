## Shroudly 2.5.10

- **Autopilot: Shroudly fixes what it can by itself.** When something is in the way, Shroudly now does what you would otherwise have had to find in Settings, and then tells you in one line what it did:
  - **Discord** blocked on your network, with the Discord app installed (Stable, PTB, Canary or Development): Discord app mode is turned on, and a Discord stuck on *Checking for updates* is restarted. A Discord that already works is left alone, so a call is never cut.
  - **Roblox** blocked on your network: Games and apps mode is turned on. A Roblox player, or a Roblox installer, that was started around Shroudly (for example from a browser that was already open) is started again through Shroudly. This works with Bloxstrap-style launchers too, whatever their name.
  - **A site that no technique opens**, or one that connects and is then cut: Advanced mode is turned on (Windows and Linux). Shroudly says why first, and your system asks for permission. If you say no, it does not ask again by itself for two weeks.
  - **Auto mode or encrypted DNS switched off** while a site is blocked: they are switched back on.

  Shroudly finds out whether Discord or Roblox is blocked before you open them: it looks at the network's DNS answer and at a direct connection, and it counts a block only when the same site works through Shroudly. A service that is simply down is never mistaken for a blocked one. A switch you turn off yourself stays off for two weeks. The **Open Roblox** and **Install Roblox** buttons and the "turn this on?" offers are gone; they are no longer needed.
- **Roblox Studio.** Studio now goes through Shroudly like the player, and so does its installer. Shroudly never closes Studio itself, because it may hold work that is not saved. If Studio was opened before Shroudly could take it through, Shroudly asks you to save your work and reopen it. That Studio really goes through Shroudly is tested on every change: Studio's real installer and Studio itself run on a Windows machine, and every connection they make must pass through Shroudly.
- **Security updates.** The packaging tool (electron-builder 26) and other build-time dependencies are updated, as are the GitHub Actions that build the releases.
- **Text.** No message names a country any more. Several translations now read more naturally: Brazilian Portuguese, Indonesian, Uzbek, Tajik, Turkmen, Ukrainian, Hindi, Amharic, Bengali, Burmese, Kazakh, Kyrgyz, Russian, Belarusian, Arabic, Persian and Japanese.

Everything from [2.5.9](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/releases/tag/v2.5.9) is included. Users of 2.0.4 and later update automatically.
