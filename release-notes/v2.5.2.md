## Shroudly 2.5.2

- **No more "Tor is running" when it is not.** Shroudly looks for other tools that steer the same traffic and warns about them. Any program whose name merely ended in "tor" (Monitor, Navigator, Creator and many more that come with Windows and drivers) was taken for Tor. Now only Tor itself and Tor Browser count. Thanks for the report in [#7](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/issues/7).
- **Clash of Clans is not a proxy client.** The same kind of check took the game, and anything else with "clash" or "xray" in its name, for the V2Ray/Clash family.

Everything from [2.5.0](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/releases/tag/v2.5.0) is included. Users of 2.0.4 and later update automatically.
