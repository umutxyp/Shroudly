## Shroudly 2.6.58

**Discord and other services with several domains open faster, Advanced mode can start with Shroudly (at Windows sign-in too), and on Android stopping hands the phone back at once.** GitHub only: the App Store and Google Play are reviewing 2.6.57, and these go to them with the next store version.

### Faster
- **Services with several domains:** Discord opens `discord.com`, `gateway.discord.gg`, `cdn.discordapp.com`, `media.discordapp.net` and `discord.media` one after another while its loading screen waits. Shroudly already shared what one name of a site learned with that site's other names (`www.`, `api.`, `cdn.`), but these are different domains, so each one searched for its own way through on first contact. Now the domains of one service count as one site, so what works for the first is used for the others at once. The same goes for YouTube (`googlevideo.com`, `ytimg.com`), X (`twimg.com`), Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, Telegram, Roblox, Twitch, Spotify, Reddit, TikTok and Signal. A name's own result still comes first, and a way that fails on one of them is dropped for all. On computers and Android, and in the iPhone .ipa here (one engine); the App Store apps get it with their next version.
- **The Discord app's start (Discord app mode):** Discord asks for its update manifest and then a dozen modules one after another before it opens. Shroudly's relay opened a new connection, with a TLS handshake through the engine, for each of them. It now keeps its connections to Discord open between requests: four requests in a row use one connection.

### Advanced mode
- **Start with Shroudly** (Settings, under Advanced mode): when this is on, Advanced mode comes on with protection every time Shroudly starts, including when it starts at Windows sign-in. Until now it came back only after a Stop and Start in the same run. Windows asks for permission each time (a packet driver needs it), never on top of a full-screen game, and not beside a game's anti-cheat or after an antivirus blocked it.

### Android
- **Stop (#78):** the tunnel and the VPN interface now go first when you stop Shroudly; its goodbye to the live count is sent after. Traffic leaves straight, or through the next VPN app, the moment protection stops, instead of up to a second and a half later.
- **Automation apps (Tasker, MacroDroid):** a macro sending Stop and then Start, or Toggle twice, could reach Shroudly while it was still stopping, and the Start was dropped. Toggle now counts "stopping" as off, and a Start waits for the stop to finish and then turns protection on.

### Releases
- The Apple release job revokes the temporary development certificate it gets from Apple on each run, so they no longer pile up to Apple's limit.
