## Shroudly 2.6.66

**Apple's own tabs on iPhone, iPad and the Mac (Liquid Glass on iOS and macOS 26), Shroudly's logo as the Protection tab on every device, a softer light round the switch, and downloads that keep working while GitHub is down.**

### iPhone, iPad and the Mac App Store app
- The tabs are now the system's own: on iPhone the bar along the bottom, in Liquid Glass on iOS 26 (press and slide along it as in Apple's apps; it draws in while a page scrolls down), on iPad a bar that turns into a sidebar, on the Mac the sidebar. Earlier iOS and macOS versions get the system's tabs as they draw them.
- Mac: hiding the sidebar narrows the window by its width instead of widening the page, and showing it widens the window back, the page staying as it was.

### Every device
- The Protection tab's icon is Shroudly's own logo, as large as the icons beside it: on the desktop, Android and Android TV, iPhone and iPad, the Mac's sidebar and Apple TV.
- The light round the switch while protected fades out softly, with no edge or rings (desktop, Android, Apple devices).

### Downloads and updates
- When github.com's download address fails (as it did for everyone on 8 November), Shroudly's own download host still serves the files: it takes them through GitHub's API, and while GitHub does not answer at all it keeps the newest version it knows. Updates on computers fall back to that host while protection is on.
- codeshare.me's Shroudly page: the button at the top downloads the build for your device (system, processor, Linux distribution; one APK for Android phones and TVs), and every download there comes from Shroudly's own host, never sent on to GitHub, which some networks block.
