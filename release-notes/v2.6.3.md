## Shroudly 2.6.3

**Telegram and other apps with their own protocol, sleep and wake, and an app that tells you why.**

### Telegram and apps that are not web traffic
- **Telegram Desktop on Windows now goes through Shroudly.** Programs that ask Windows' WinHTTP for the proxy (Telegram Desktop, .NET programs, BITS) never reached Shroudly before; they do now.
- **Apps with a protocol of their own** (Telegram's MTProto and the like) get Shroudly's techniques too, when a network recognises them by their first packets. The technique that works is remembered for that server.
- **An honest word when nothing can help:** if a network blocks an app's server addresses themselves, Shroudly says so in one line, names the app, and tells you that the app's own proxy setting or a VPN is the way. It never keeps retrying a dead address, and it says nothing while you are simply offline.
- Local network devices (printers, NAS, routers) and company or VPN names are never touched.

### Sleep, wake and restarts
- **Waking the computer no longer restarts protection.** After sleep, a lid close or a clock change, Shroudly no longer mistakes the pause for a stuck engine, so connections and downloads in progress (a Discord update, for example) are not cut.
- **Nothing goes around Shroudly while the engine restarts:** programs wait a moment and go through it, and the system proxy stays in place.
- On Android, sleep (Doze) and clock changes never end protection, and with Android's "Block connections without VPN" Shroudly keeps retrying instead of leaving the phone offline.

### Shroudly tells you why
- When Advanced mode, Discord app mode, Games and apps mode or Microsoft Store apps would help but are not turned on (declined, turned off by hand, no administrator, a game's anti-cheat running), Shroudly now says so in one line and tells you what to do, instead of staying silent.
- When another program changes the system proxy, the log now says what it was changed to and which program it was, when that can be told.
- A site that cannot be reached is logged once, then at most every half hour, not every minute.
- Explanations no longer name any country.

### Copy logs
- **Copy logs** in the Logs panel and the tray menu puts the whole log, with your version and system, on the clipboard, ready to paste into a report. Nothing is sent anywhere. On Android, **Share logs** under About now tells a little more.

Everything from [2.6.2](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/releases/tag/v2.6.2) is included. Desktop users update automatically; the Android app tells you that 2.6.3 is out.
