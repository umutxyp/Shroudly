## Shroudly 2.6.73

**Apps always through Tor: pick an app such as Instagram or Telegram and all of it goes through Tor. An app whose servers the network blocks goes through Tor too, and the Tor question names it.**

### Desktop, Android, Android TV and the Mac
- **Apps always through Tor** (Connections by site). A user in Iran asked for Instagram always through Tor. Until now only a site could be sent through Tor, one name at a time; an app uses many (Instagram: instagram.com, cdninstagram.com, fbcdn.net and more) and, like Telegram, may connect to its servers by address. Now twelve apps can be picked as a whole: Instagram, Telegram, WhatsApp, YouTube, X, Facebook, Discord, Signal, Reddit, TikTok, Spotify and ChatGPT. A picked app's names go through Tor, and for Telegram its address ranges too (the engine now takes `TOR <address>/<bits>`), blocked or not. Picking one is the consent to Tor, as sending a site through it is. A name two apps share (fbcdn.net: Instagram's and Facebook's pictures) goes back the usual way only once neither is picked. Shown wherever this copy has Tor: not in the lite APK, on iPhone or on Apple TV.
- A site or app sent through Tor that was opened while Tor was still starting kept its connection made the usual way (a browser keeps one for minutes), so it did not go through Tor until much later. Now, the moment Tor is ready, those connections end once and the app connects again through Tor. Checked on Android: www.instagram.com went the usual way before, and through Tor after.
- When the list sent through Tor is given anew (as the Mac app does at every change), what left it is no longer kept through Tor.

### Desktop and Android
- An app that connects to its servers by address (Telegram, WhatsApp, Signal and others) can find those addresses blocked outright, as Telegram's are in Iran. Tor already carried such connections once allowed, but Shroudly said the opposite: a notice to use "the app's own proxy setting or a VPN", which a user there cannot do. Now, where this copy has Tor: allowed, the app's connections simply go through it; not allowed yet, Shroudly asks, naming the app; turned off, it asks again now and then, as it does for sites. The old notice is left only where there is no Tor (the lite APK) or your own server is set.

### Desktop, Android and the Mac
- The Tor question named whatever needed Tor first, often a bare address ("Open 2001:4188:…:35 through Tor?"), and kept that even when Telegram came next, so nobody knew what they were being asked about. Now it names the app when the address is a known one (Telegram for 149.154.167.92 or t.me), keeps a site's name over a later address, and puts the better name in the notification at once.

### How it looks (every screen checked on Apple TV, iPhone, Android and the desktop)
- Apple TV: the notice before the first start was a small box with its text cut off and Continue out of sight; it now fills the screen, readable from the couch, with Continue in focus.
- Apple TV: Settings, Follow and About ran their rows behind the tabs along the top when scrolled; the list now ends below them. About's last paragraph (Conduit) could not be reached with the remote; now it can. "Something not working?" is laid out like the other pages instead of stretched across the TV.
- Apple TV: the start button was read out by VoiceOver as "MarkClosed" (a picture's file name); it now says Start protection or Stop protection.
- Apple TV and iPhone: What's new showed a note about the Mac; the Mac now has its own notes.
- Android: the buttons on Connections by site (Through Tor, Block) and the report's Send were the system's grey slabs in capitals; they now have the app's look, and Send is faded until it can be pressed.
