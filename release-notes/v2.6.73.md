## Shroudly 2.6.73

**An app whose servers the network blocks (Telegram in Iran) goes through Tor, and the Tor question names it.**

### Desktop, Android and Android TV
- An app that connects to its servers by address (Telegram, WhatsApp, Signal and others) can find those addresses blocked outright, as Telegram's are in Iran. Tor already carried such connections once allowed, but Shroudly said the opposite: a notice to use "the app's own proxy setting or a VPN", which a user there cannot do. Now, where this copy has Tor: allowed, the app's connections simply go through it; not allowed yet, Shroudly asks, naming the app; turned off, it asks again now and then, as it does for sites. The old notice is left only where there is no Tor (the lite APK) or your own server is set.
- The Tor question named whatever needed Tor first, often a bare address ("Open 2001:4188:…:35 through Tor?"), and kept that even when Telegram came next, so nobody knew what they were being asked about. Now it names the app when the address is a known one (Telegram for 149.154.167.92 or t.me), keeps a site's name over a later address, and puts the better name in the notification at once.

### Mac (App Store)
- The Tor question names the app the same way (such as Telegram) instead of an address.
