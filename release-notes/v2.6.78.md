## Shroudly 2.6.78

**Ad blocking says what it stopped and lets a site through; your own sites join the automatic list; the icon you choose shows everywhere, and the colour can follow it.**

### Desktop
- Connections lists what ad blocking stopped lately, with **Let through** beside each name; reports list them too. A page the list blocks whole (a link shortener's, say) opened only with protection off, and nothing said why.
- **Only listed sites**: the sites in the box are added to the automatic list instead of replacing it. One edit used to freeze the list at the built-in sites, so a site blocked since (LinkedIn on one network: reset at the first try, open after a few reloads) never went through Shroudly. Lists kept that way keep only the names that are on neither list.
- The icon you choose shows in the notification area and the menu bar (dimmed while off) and on the power button. Back to the classic icon, the Dock shows it at once instead of after a restart; the chosen icon is put back whenever macOS redraws the Dock tile.
- **Colour follows the icon** (Settings, Appearance): the app's colour matches the chosen icon; picking a colour turns it off.
- With Tor turned off and a site that only Tor opens, Shroudly says so in the window; the question about turning it on comes back after a day instead of a week.

### Android
- **Only listed sites** with nothing listed works on every site again; it opened nothing (a report from Iran).
- Connections lists what ad blocking stopped (the GitHub APK), with **Let through**.
- The header shows the chosen icon, and **Colour follows the icon** is in Settings.
- With Tor turned off, a site no technique opens asks about Tor again after a day, for cut and unreachable sites too, not only blocked ones.

### iPhone, iPad and the Mac
- The header and About show the chosen icon, and **Colour follows the icon** is in Settings.
- Mac: with Tor turned off, the question comes back after a day; a site cut or unreachable counts as well as a blocked one.
