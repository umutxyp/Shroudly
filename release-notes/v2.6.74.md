## Shroudly 2.6.74

**Faster again where Tor took a site once; Tor that works in Russia (it waits for slow bridges, and it can be fetched there); Gemini and AI Studio through Tor.**

### Desktop, Android and the Mac
- "Since the Tor feature it always uses Tor, and it is very slow." A name Tor carried was kept on Tor for 30 minutes, and every connection Tor carried started the 30 minutes again, so a site that fell to Tor once (a moment the network failed, a wake from sleep) stayed on Tor as long as it was used and never tried the usual ways again. Now it stays there 15 minutes from the first time, then the techniques are tried again; only if they still fail does it go back to Tor.
- Tor through bridges (Russia: webtunnel, meek) can take longer than 15 seconds to reach a site; the engine gave up at 15, every time, so Telegram never went through it even with Tor ready ("does not speak the protocol chosen", 0 connections carried). It now waits 45 seconds for Tor, and says "did not reach the site in time" when that is what happened.
- Gemini and AI Studio in "Apps always through Tor": their pages and the APIs the prompts go to, together. Sending some Google names through Tor by hand and not others (an Iran report) opened AI Studio but nothing could be written.

### Desktop
- Tor is fetched the first time it is needed from torproject.org, which Russia cuts: Tor was never fetched there, and the log said "ENOENT bundle.tar.gz" instead of why. The same files are now also on Shroudly's GitHub (release `tor-16.0`), tried next, and checked against the same SHA-256 as the Tor Project's, so only their file is ever used; a cut connection is said as such.
