## Shroudly 2.6.38

**Support Shroudly: PayPal, or a bank transfer from your own country.**

Shroudly is free, with no ads and no tracking, and it will stay that way. Donations pay for its servers, code signing and the work of keeping up with new blocks. We share what comes in and what it is spent on.

### In the app
- **About → Support Shroudly** (desktop, and the APK from GitHub on Android): a PayPal button with its QR code, and the bank account for the country you send from, with a copy button on every line. Shroudly picks the account from your system's region (on Android, from your SIM's country first); another country can be chosen.
- Local transfers in TRY, GBP, EUR (SEPA), USD, AUD, CAD, NZD, SGD, HKD, PHP (with QR Ph) and HUF. From anywhere else, an international (Swift) transfer to the GBP account, which takes them from more than 150 countries.
- The desktop tray menu has *Support Shroudly* too.
- Now and then, while protection is on, a small card on the main screen mentions it: first after three days, and after *Not now* not again for two months. It never opens a window or sends a notification, and it does not appear in countries where neither PayPal nor these banks can take a donation.
- Copies of the Android app installed from Google Play show none of this, as Play requires.

### On GitHub
- Every account is in the README under [Support Shroudly](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly#support-shroudly), and the repository has a Sponsor button.

Everything from [2.6.37](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/releases/tag/v2.6.37) is included. Desktop users update automatically; the Android app tells you that 2.6.38 is out.
