## Shroudly 2.6.25

**When another program is doing the same job and getting in the way, Shroudly names it instead of blaming the network.**

### Two bypass tools at once
- Advanced mode changes the first packets of a connection, through the one facility each system has for it (WinDivert on Windows, NFQUEUE on Linux). Other tools — GoodbyeDPI, zapret, ByeDPI and the like — do the same thing the same way. Run two at once and they undo each other: nothing fails out loud, Advanced mode reports itself active, and the sites still don't open. One reporter lost a day to GoodbyeDPI left running beside Shroudly.
- Worse, Shroudly could then decide the network was blocking the site's addresses and tell you only a VPN would help — when the real cause was on your own machine.
- Shroudly now looks for such a program (by name only — it reads nothing of another program's traffic, files or memory, and needs no extra permission) and names it: when Advanced mode comes on, and in place of the only-a-VPN message when a service can't be reached. It never turns anything off; which of the two to keep is your choice.

Everything from [2.6.24](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/releases/tag/v2.6.24) is included. Desktop users update automatically; the Android app tells you that 2.6.25 is out.
