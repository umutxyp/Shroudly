## Shroudly 2.6.67

**Roblox's maps, items and pictures load again where they had started to go missing after a while, protection no longer ends by itself on some phones, and Tor is no longer started for nothing.**

### Every device (the engine)
- Games and apps that close connections they no longer need (Roblox above all) were taken for "the app refused the certificate", and the engine let go of a technique that worked, one after another; since 2.6.62 it also kept that across restarts. Parts of a game (a map, some weapons) then stopped loading after a while, until the next day. Now only a technique that changes what the server is given can be blamed, a connection closed unused is never a refusal, and what was wrongly kept is dropped.
- When Tor carried one name of a service, every other domain of that service (Roblox's rbxcdn.com beside roblox.com, YouTube's googlevideo.com) went to Tor after a few seconds as well, where it crawls. Now only names of the same domain do; the others keep trying the techniques.
- A name that does not exist (a phone maker's retired telemetry address, for one) no longer starts Tor.

### Your own server (Android, iPhone, iPad, Mac App Store app)
- A web page could still read this device's own address beside the server's through WebRTC (browserleaks.com/webrtc; issues #58, #59, #67): WebRTC's address check (a STUN request) left over UDP from the device. With your own server set it is no longer sent out, so WebRTC goes through the server; games and calls on their own UDP go on as before.

### Android and Android TV
- A phone could lose its protection again and again ("the tunnel keeps failing: it takes apps' packets and gives nothing back") while connections were going through. Connections reaching the engine now count as the tunnel working.

### iPhone and iPad
- Settings: the technique on the Connection tab is no longer stuck right under the tabs.
