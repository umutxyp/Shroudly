## Shroudly 2.6.70

**Tor gets through where its usual bridges are blocked (Russia), and a shorter report form.**

### The last way out (Tor): desktop, Android, Mac
- Where Snowflake and the bridges that come with the app are blocked (Russia's Beeline, for one), Tor never connected. Now it asks for the bridges the Tor Project gives for the user's own country (WebTunnel and obfs4 that are in no app's list) and tries those next. Every country is asked the same way; where the Tor Project has nothing particular for one, its newest general bridges come instead.
- Once those bridges got Tor through, the next start goes to them first.

### "Something not working?"
- No internet provider or country to fill in any more: the log already says what is needed.
- One box instead of two: send the report with the app's log and accept the privacy policy, with the policy's link right under it.

### Android and Android TV
- "Tap to start" and the status at the top were set again every second: a screen reader (TalkBack) could read them out over and over. Now only when they change.
