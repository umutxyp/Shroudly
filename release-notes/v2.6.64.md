## Shroudly 2.6.64

**Shroudly's own logo in the middle, its eye shut while off and open once on; the logo itself on the taskbar, the tray, the menu bar and the Dock; on phones the round switch is the button, with tabs you press and slide along; a site that turns your country away can be sent through Tor; and on phones too, only the sites you list can go through Shroudly.** On GitHub only for now; the App Store and Google Play have 2.6.61 in review, and their testers get this version.

### The round switch: Shroudly's logo
- The circle in the middle of Protection now holds Shroudly's logo, exactly as at the window's top left: shut and grey while protection is off, open and in its own colours once it is on, the one fading into the other. Calm: no rings or spinning light; a still glow while on and a thin arc only while starting or stopping. The same on Windows, macOS, Linux, iPhone, iPad, the Mac App Store app, Android and both TVs.
- Phones and tablets (iPhone, iPad, Android): the circle is the button. A tap starts or stops protection, with a tick of the phone, and *Tap to start* / *Tap to stop* under it. Computers and the Mac App Store app keep the **Start protection** button under it; Android TV and Apple TV keep a button for the remote.

### Icons: the logo itself everywhere
- The notification area and the menu bar show Shroudly's logo, its eye open while protection is on and shut in grey while off (they were a separately drawn line icon). Windows gets every size a display scale asks for.
- The window's icon on the taskbar (Windows, Linux) and the notifications' icon were a blurred 64-pixel copy of the logo; they are the logo at full size now.
- The app icon (Dock, Launchpad, iPhone and iPad home screen, Mac App Store app, Apple TV) is the logo large on the app's own dark background, without the glass effect that changed its look.
- Android: the status bar, the notification, the Quick Settings tile and the themed launcher icon show the logo in one colour, its rim the same width all round.

### Phones: made for phones
- iPhone and iPad: the tabs float at the bottom on Liquid Glass (frosted glass before iOS 26). Press and slide along them: a lens follows the finger with a light tick at each tab, and letting go opens it; or swipe the page sideways to the tab beside it.
- Android: the same floating tabs with the lens that follows the finger, and a sideways swipe to the tab beside it.
- On phones Logs is a row of About instead of a tab, so the other tabs keep room for their names; computers, the Mac and the TVs keep it as a tab.
- Settings' four tabs stay on one line while their names fit whole, else go two by two; never shrunk or cut. Stats' counters sit side by side while each has the room its words need and go one under the other once they would be squeezed (a large text size, a long language, a narrow phone).
- Android Stats now has *Online now* as a card, live download and upload speeds with their last minute drawn, and the counters as on the desktop (connections, got past the block, data carried, time on).
- Apple TV: the system's tabs along the top, icons only; Stats in large tiles; Settings, Follow and About laid out as the TV's own Settings app.
- The tabs at the bottom show their icons alone on every device (the name is the tooltip and what screen readers say).

### Send a site through Tor
- A site that turns your country away itself (it says it is not available where you are: NVIDIA's AI pages, some AI chats and shops in Iran) is not censorship, so no technique opens it; only an address elsewhere does. In **Stats → Connections by site**, **Through Tor** beside a site (or typed into the search box) now sends that site and every name under it through Tor; **Usual way** brings it back. Choosing it is your yes to Tor: the last way out is turned on if it was off. Computers, Android (the full APK) and the Mac App Store app; not with your own server, which already carries everything.

### Only the sites you list, on phones too
- Computers already had **Only listed sites**. Android, iPhone, iPad and the Mac App Store app now have it too: **Settings → Connection → Only listed sites**, one site per line (a site covers every name under it). Only those go through Shroudly's ways; every other site goes out as it is, with your network's own DNS. Android keeps choosing apps as before (**Apps that bypass Shroudly**, or only the apps you pick).

### Google Play
- The bundle for Google Play carries a newer AndroidX Fragment library (1.3.6): Play had flagged the 1.0.0 that its in-app update library brings in as outdated.

### Reports
- Reports say how many sites you send through Tor and whether only listed sites are on.
- A report's list of connections now keeps the addresses apps connect to (a VPN app chained through **Proxy only**, a game, a messenger's fallback) and anything refused; it showed names only, so such an app's connections never appeared. Computers and Android.
- Android, **Proxy only**: turning it off now says how many connections went through it, as protection does, so a report tells an app that never came to Shroudly from one that did.
