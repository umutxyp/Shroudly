## Shroudly 2.4.9

- **Menus and toasts are solid with glass on.** The language list, and other dropdowns, toasts and the welcome tour, let the settings behind them show through, so their text ran into what was underneath. They are now opaque, in every colour.
- **Notices no longer stick to the header.** The update notice and the "restart Discord" kind of notice sat right against the header line. They now have the same room above them as below.
- **The update notice is never cut off.** "Restarting for the update in 54 s" lost its end behind the buttons. The text is now shown in full; when the window is too narrow for both, the buttons move to a line of their own.

Everything from [2.4.8](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/releases/tag/v2.4.8) is included. Users of 2.0.4 and later update automatically.
