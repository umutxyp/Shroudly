## Shroudly 2.6.42

**Android: moving between Wi-Fi and mobile data no longer stops everything until protection is turned off and on.**

- When the phone went from Wi-Fi to mobile data, or back, with protection on, apps and lookups could hang until protection was turned off and on again. This showed most when the old network did not go away at once: Wi-Fi out of range but still joined, or mobile data staying up for a while after Wi-Fi came back.
- The cause: what was opened over the old network stayed in use, though nothing came through it any more. That covered both apps' connections and Shroudly's own encrypted DNS connections, including to a DNS server of your own. Without a VPN, Android ends an app's connections when their network goes. Shroudly's VPN stays across the change, so the apps never found out.
- Now, as soon as the phone is on another network, Shroudly lets go of everything opened over the old one:
  - Encrypted DNS connects anew and forgets the waits it earned there, as the desktop app already did.
  - Apps' connections through Shroudly are ended, so the apps connect again over the new network at once.
- A DNS server of your own (Settings → DNS server) connects again on the new network too.

Everything from [2.6.41](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/releases/tag/v2.6.41) is included. Desktop users update automatically; the Android app tells you that 2.6.42 is out.
