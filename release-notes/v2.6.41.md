## Shroudly 2.6.41

**Android: choose which apps go through Shroudly, and turn QUIC or IPv6 off where your network blocks them (#59, #60).**

### Only the apps you choose ([#60](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/issues/60))
- About → Settings → Apps now works both ways. As before, every app goes through Shroudly except the ones you check. Or, the other way round, only the apps you check go through Shroudly, and every other app, system apps too, goes straight out. Pick your browser and messengers, and nothing else is touched.
- Each way keeps its own list, so switching back finds the other one as you left it. With nothing checked, every app goes through Shroudly.

### QUIC off, IPv6 off ([#59](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/issues/59))
- New in About → Settings → Network, both off by default.
- **QUIC off:** apps use TCP instead of QUIC (UDP port 443). On networks that block QUIC or slow it down, apps no longer try it first, and TCP is what Shroudly's techniques get through.
- **IPv6 off:** the phone's apps and Shroudly use IPv4 only. Apps are told at once that there is no IPv6 instead of waiting for it. For networks whose IPv6 is broken or slow.
- On the computer, browsers going through Shroudly do not use QUIC anyway, so these two are Android settings.

Everything from [2.6.40](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/releases/tag/v2.6.40) is included. Desktop users update automatically; the Android app tells you that 2.6.41 is out.
