## Shroudly 2.6.43

**Android: ready for Google Play.**

- Shroudly is on its way to Google Play. The Play copy is the same app, signed with the same key, so you can move between the GitHub APK and Play without uninstalling or losing your settings.
- Play's rules do not allow an app to block other apps' ads, so in a copy that Google Play installed or updates, **Block ads and trackers** is not offered and blocks nothing. As before, that copy does not show the donation section and gets its updates from Play, not from GitHub.
- The APK from GitHub is unchanged: ad and tracker blocking, the donation section and update checks stay as they were.

Everything from [2.6.42](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/releases/tag/v2.6.42) is included. Desktop users update automatically; the Android app tells you that 2.6.43 is out.
