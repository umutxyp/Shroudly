## Shroudly 2.6.69

**Sites that Russia's filters freeze partway through now open through Tor on the next try.**

### Every device (the engine)
- In Russia, connections to Cloudflare's addresses and many other hosts abroad freeze after about 16 KB, whatever technique is used, since it is the address that is filtered, not the name. Shroudly waited for three such broken pages before it sent the site through Tor (the last way out); now the very next connection goes there (freeip.me on T2, for one).

### Reports
- Reports from a phone whose time zone is set wrong (a right wall clock, but hours off in UTC) were refused as "bad" by the report server. Fixed on the server, so no update is needed for that.
