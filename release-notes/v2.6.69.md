## Shroudly 2.6.69

**Instagram, WhatsApp and Facebook on Iran's networks with IPv6, Instagram's Reels, sites Russia's filters freeze partway through, and reports that say what to put right.**

### Every device (the engine)
- Iran's networks answer a blocked name with their block page, and on IPv6 that address (2001:4188:2:600:10:10:34:3x) was taken for the site's own: where encrypted DNS is blocked too, Instagram, WhatsApp and Facebook went to the block page instead of the techniques or Tor (MCI, Irancell). IPv6 block pages and addresses no public site has are now refused, as IPv4 ones were.
- An app that gives up on a connection in two or three seconds (Instagram, for its Reels) never waited long enough for the techniques to end and Tor to take over, so that name never got a byte while other names of the same site worked through Tor. Now, after a few, such a name goes straight to Tor too.
- In Russia, connections to Cloudflare's addresses and many other hosts abroad freeze after about 16 KB, whatever technique is used. Shroudly waited for three broken pages before sending the site through Tor; now the very next connection goes there (freeip.me, for one).

### Android and Android TV
- The log says where a Stop came from (the window, the notification, the quick settings tile, the widget, another app), so a report shows whether protection was turned off or went off by itself.

### "Something not working?"
- A report now needs what happened in a few real sentences (at least 80 characters), the site or app, and an e-mail address for the answer. Telegram names, made-up and throwaway addresses aren't accepted; the app says what to put right as you type.
- In Iran the link shows only when Shroudly itself has gone wrong (it could not start, protection ended by itself, the engine got stuck), for a day; most reports from there were about addresses blocked outright, which no app on the phone changes.
- Reports from a phone whose time zone is set wrong were refused by the report server; fixed there, no update needed for that one.
