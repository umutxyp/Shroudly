## Shroudly 2.4.6

- **Window buttons blend in again with glass on.** On Windows and Linux, the strip behind the close, maximise and minimise buttons kept the solid theme's colour when glass was on, and stood out as a separate block, most visibly over the accent glow. It now matches the title bar in every accent colour, glass on or off. The buttons themselves are the system's own, unchanged.

Everything from [2.4.5](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/releases/tag/v2.4.5) is included. Users of 2.0.4 and later update automatically.
