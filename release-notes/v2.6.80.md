## Shroudly 2.6.80

**QUIC turned off is refused on the spot on every system; the country is read from the time zone first; "Something not working?" only after Shroudly itself went wrong; a Roblox installer from the browser goes through Shroudly by itself; Tor 16.0.1.**

### Every system
- QUIC turned off (the default on phones, "QUIC to TCP" in the desktop's Advanced mode): an app's or a program's QUIC Initial is now answered on the spot with a Version Negotiation that offers no real version, so it gives QUIC up at once and uses TCP. Dropped unanswered, apps first waited for QUIC's own timeout: Instagram's comments opened late, or minutes later, in Turkey. On Android, iPhone, iPad and the Mac the engine answers it; on Windows and Linux in Advanced mode the packet layer does. Nothing of it goes out to the network, as before.
- The country Shroudly takes you to be in (its decoy names, the national-network check) is now read from the time zone first, and only then from a shutdown seen before; a site of another country answering alone no longer counts as a national-only network. One had made a phone in Turkey "Iranian" for good.

- "Something not working?" shows only after Shroudly itself went wrong (protection could not start, the engine ended or stuck, apps or browsers not getting through it), for a day, and goes again once a report is sent; in every country. A network down, a blocked address or a refused permission does not count.
- Tor 16.0.1 (was 16.0) on Windows, macOS, Linux and Android, with the Snowflake and bridge fixes it brings.

### Desktop
- A Roblox installer started from the browser, before Roblox is installed, goes around the system proxy; on a network that blocks Roblox (by DNS or otherwise) it failed. Shroudly now checks at once when it sees one running, turns Games and apps mode on where Roblox is blocked, and starts the installer again through itself.

### Android, iPhone, iPad and the Mac
- When the system ends Shroudly to save memory or battery while protection is on and starts it again, the log now says so; a report showed only the engine starting over and over (Roblox during a WhatsApp call).
- Android: the explanation under "Only listed sites" is behind its title's (i), as every other section's, and About no longer repeats Settings (the bar's gear opens them; issue #47). The Play build carries androidx.fragment 1.9.1.
