## Shroudly 2.6.60

**"Something not working?" on every Shroudly: tell us what does not work right from the main screen, and it reaches the developer with the app's log in one go. Android TV no longer loses protection on networks that let no UDP through, and Advanced in Android's settings now opens where you can see it.** Also sent to the App Store and Google Play.

### Something not working? (all apps)
- **Under the main button**, on computers (Windows, macOS, Linux), Android phones, tablets and TVs, iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV. Write what does not work, where, and what you were doing (50 to 1000 characters); the site or app, your internet provider, country and an email or Telegram name for an answer are optional.
- **The log goes with it**, so there is nothing to copy or attach: the app's recent log, the version, the system and device model, and what the app shows right now (whether protection is on and working, the engine's counters, the settings: switches and choices only, never a server's link, an address or a password). Before **Send**, the app says exactly what goes and where, and Send waits until you agree to send it, log included.
- **A reference number** comes back (`SR-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`): quote it if you write to us. The numbers you sent stay listed on your device for 60 days, then go by themselves.
- **Nothing is kept on our servers:** the report goes to `codeshare.me`, which passes it to the developer by email and forgets it; it is never written to disk or a database there, and only its reference is logged. The country Cloudflare saw and the internet provider the address belongs to are added to the email; the address itself is not. The [privacy policy](https://codeshare.me/projects/shroudly/privacy) says it in full.
- **No spam, no attacks:** one report an hour and three a day from a device; on the server, a limit per network and for everyone together; every report carries a proof of work bound to its own text (a fraction of a second for the app, a cost for anyone trying to send many from a script), a copy of one is refused, and everything in it is checked, cleaned and mailed as plain text only.

### Android TV and phones: networks that let no UDP through
- On such a network (an Android TV in Iran, for one), apps that send only UDP for a while (QUIC from YouTube, a call, a game) got nothing back, and Shroudly's check of its tunnel took that for the tunnel being stuck: it restarted it, and the second time within ten minutes turned protection off with "the tunnel keeps failing: it takes apps' packets and gives nothing back". UDP the engine receives now counts as the tunnel working, as answers do.

### Android: Settings → Advanced
- Advanced opened at the very end of the page, below the fold and all at once, so a tap on it looked like it did nothing. It now unfolds with an animation and the page moves up to it.
