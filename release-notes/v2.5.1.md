## Shroudly 2.5.1

- **The line under the title bar now runs the full width on Windows and Linux.** It stopped short under the minimise, maximise and close buttons, because the strip the system draws behind those buttons covered its last few pixels. It now sits just below that strip. macOS was not affected.

Everything from [2.4.8](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/releases/tag/v2.4.8) is included. Users of 2.0.4 and later update automatically.
