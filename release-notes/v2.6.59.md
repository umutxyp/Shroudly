## Shroudly 2.6.59

**Your own server keeps DNS and QUIC on it, the address sites see shows its country and flag, YouTube's image and video names are reported as blocked again, the Android Lite APK leaves out everything of Tor's, and League of Legends' names share what works.** Also sent to the App Store and Google Play.

### Your own server
- **No more DNS leaving from your own connection (#67, #59):** with a server of your own, an app's DNS question that encrypted DNS did not answer within 2.5 seconds, or had no route for, was sent on in plaintext from your own connection, and a DNS leak test then showed your provider beside the server. A question to the router's resolver went to the router too, which asks your provider in turn. With a server of your own, every DNS question now goes through encrypted DNS and the server, or gets no answer and is asked again; only a name only your network knows (`printer.lan`) still goes to the router.
- **QUIC over the server:** QUIC (UDP to port 443: YouTube, Instagram, Google) left from your own address, past the server. With a server of your own it is now dropped, so browsers and apps use TCP, which goes through the server. Other UDP (games, calls) goes on as before.
- **Its country (#60):** next to the address sites see, on the main screen and in the server test in Settings: the country's flag and name, in the app's language (`80.174.118.60 · 🇮🇹 Italy`). On computers, Android, and the iPhone .ipa here.

### YouTube's images and videos (#79)
- Since 2.6.58 one service's domains share what works, but they also shared the verdict "the rest of the site works, so this name is not blocked". When `i.ytimg.com` or `googlevideo.com` was blocked on its own addresses while `youtube.com` opened, Shroudly said "not a block" and neither told you nor offered Advanced mode. That verdict is now the domain's own again: those names are reported, and handled, as blocked.

### Automation apps (Android)
- 2.6.58 made a Start sent while Shroudly is still stopping wait for the stop, but the start could then reach the service just as it ended, and it failed ("Could not start protection"). A start that comes in while a stop finishes now keeps the service.

### Android Lite (#75)
- The Lite APK has no Tor, and now nothing of Tor's is offered there either: no "Help through this device" (a Snowflake proxy for the Tor network) in Settings, it never runs there whatever an earlier copy kept, and the "help others" card offers Psiphon's Conduit without Orbot. Lite's settings also lose a doubled divider line.

### League of Legends (#76)
- Riot's names (`riotgames.com`, `leagueoflegends.com`, `pvp.net`, `riotcdn.net`, `playvalorant.com`, `lolstatic.com`) count as one site, as Discord's do since 2.6.58: what works for one is used for the others at once, the login page `xsso.leagueoflegends.com` included.
