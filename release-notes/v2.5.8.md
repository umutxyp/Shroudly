## Shroudly 2.5.8

- **Blocked sites open at once after a restart.** Shroudly used to forget which technique opened which blocked site every time it stopped: after Stop and Start, a restart of the computer or an update it searched again, and the first connections to those sites (Discord's at login, for one) could fail while it did. It now remembers what worked and starts with it. A technique learned more than half an hour earlier is still checked against the unchanged connection before it is relied on, and one that has stopped working is replaced as before, so a change of network or a lifted block costs nothing. What it remembers stays on this computer, for 30 days at most.

Everything from [2.5.7](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/releases/tag/v2.5.7) is included. Users of 2.0.4 and later update automatically.
