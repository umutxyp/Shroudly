## Shroudly 2.6.7

**Choose Shroudly's language on Android, and a bypass setting that leaves connections alone and only encrypts DNS.**

### Android: the app's language ([#38](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/issues/38))
- About → Settings → **Language**: the phone's language (as before), or any of Shroudly's 31, each listed in its own name. Handy when you report a problem in English while your phone speaks another language.
- It applies to the whole app, including its notifications. On Android 13 and later it is the same as the system's per-app language setting.

### "Off": encrypted DNS only ([#37](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/issues/37))
- A new choice in the bypass strategy list, on the desktop and on Android: **Off (encrypted DNS only)**. Connections go out exactly as apps send them and only name lookups go through Shroudly's encrypted DNS (or your own DNS server). Use it where DNS is the only block, or to tell whether a technique is getting in the way.
- **Auto (recommended)** already sends every connection untouched first, and only uses a technique when a site is actually refused. It then remembers that technique for that site.

Everything from [2.6.6](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/releases/tag/v2.6.6) is included. Desktop users update automatically; the Android app tells you that 2.6.7 is out.
