## Shroudly 2.6.1

**A new look for the Android app.**

- **The logo is the switch.** The big circle in the middle is the real Shroudly logo: tap it to turn protection on or off. Off, it is quiet and grey; on, it lights up in the logo's blues with a soft glow.
- **Colours from the logo.** Every blue on the screen now comes from the logo itself: its deep navy, its royal blue and the bright blue of its eye.
- **Cleaner screen.** Live traffic in four tiles (connections, uptime, download, upload), and release notes, source code and privacy policy in a tidy "About" card.

Nothing changes on the desktop. Everything from [2.6.0](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/releases/tag/v2.6.0) is included. Desktop users update automatically; the Android app tells you that 2.6.1 is out.
