## Shroudly 2.6.72

**Reports get through where the report server is slowed (Russia).**

### Desktop, Android and Android TV
- Russia's networks freeze connections to Cloudflare's addresses, where the report server is, after about 16 KB, and a report with its log is more than that. A report was sent through Tor only when Tor happened to be running already; with Tor off, "Something not working?" could not send anything there. Now the report goes through Tor: if Tor is not running, the app starts it just for the report (whether or not you said yes to Tor for sites), waits for it (up to two and a half minutes), sends the whole report through it, and stops it again where you had not said yes. Not where Tor is turned off in Settings.
