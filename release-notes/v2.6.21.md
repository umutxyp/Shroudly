## Shroudly 2.6.21

**Repair and Reset on the desktop, and a cleaner Settings screen on Android.**

### Desktop: Repair, and Reset all settings ([#46](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/issues/46))
- **Settings → Application → Repair** puts right what can go wrong over time, and keeps your settings:
  - It checks Shroudly's own files. If one is missing or damaged, Repair names it and links the download: installing Shroudly again over itself brings it back and keeps every setting.
  - It forgets the techniques Shroudly learned for each site and starts the engine anew.
  - It sets the system proxy again.
  - It empties the system's DNS cache.
  - It fetches the ad and tracker list again, and checks Windows' WinHTTP proxy.
- **Reset settings** puts every setting back to how a new install has it; your language stays.
  - First it turns off the modes properly, so nothing is left behind: Discord's settings, the programs' proxy, the Roblox links, the Store apps' exemptions and Advanced mode are all put back.
  - Then Shroudly restarts.

### Android: a cleaner Settings screen ([#47](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/issues/47))
- Settings now has a bar with its title and a back arrow, as any Android screen has.
- Each setting shows its title and its current value. The long explanations are hidden until you tap the **ⓘ** next to a section's name. Tap it again to hide them.

Everything from [2.6.20](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/releases/tag/v2.6.20) is included. Desktop users update automatically; the Android app tells you that 2.6.21 is out.
