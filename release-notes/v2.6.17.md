## Shroudly 2.6.17

**A site the network stops right after it opened now gets Advanced mode, and a service blocked by address is said plainly.**

### A site that opened, then went silent ([#43](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/issues/43))
- On some networks a site opens with one technique, and a minute later no technique gets any answer from it. Shroudly treated that silence like a site that is down. It only wrote a line in the log and never offered Advanced mode.
- A site that opened a moment ago is not down. When a site that a technique opened in the last half hour goes silent, Shroudly now treats it as the network stopping it. It tells you, and on Windows and Linux it turns on Advanced mode by itself, asking for permission as usual.

### A service blocked by address, said plainly
- Some networks block a service's servers by address, as some do for Roblox. No connection to those servers ever opens, and no technique on your computer can change that. Shroudly used to say this only in the log, while the app just kept waiting.
- Now Shroudly first checks the service's addresses directly. If none of them answers while the internet works, it tells you in one line that only a VPN gets the service through on this network. This is said once per network, and never when your computer is simply offline.

Everything from [2.6.16](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/releases/tag/v2.6.16) is included. Desktop users update automatically; the Android app tells you that 2.6.17 is out.
