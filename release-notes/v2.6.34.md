## Shroudly 2.6.34

**A switch for everything Shroudly would bring up on its own (#55).**

### Settings → Ask for permission by itself
- When a site or app needs Advanced mode or Microsoft Store apps, Shroudly asks for your computer's permission by itself (Windows' permission prompt). Turn this new switch off and it never does: it tells you what needs which mode, and you turn the mode on in Settings when it suits you.
- Advanced mode that stepped aside for a game's anti-cheat also stays off after the game when the switch is off, and you are told so.
- On by default, so nothing changes unless you turn it off.

### Clearer notifications setting
- **Show notifications** turns off all of Shroudly's system notifications (protection started or stopped, what it fixed by itself, updates), not only the ones about protection. Its description now says so.

Since 2.6.33 Shroudly's own window never comes up by itself, and these two switches cover everything else. Together they turn off all of Shroudly's pop-ups.

Everything from [2.6.33](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/releases/tag/v2.6.33) is included. Desktop users update automatically; the Android app tells you that 2.6.34 is out.
