## Shroudly 2.5.0

- **16 more languages, for the countries that filter the internet hardest.** Shroudly now speaks Azərbaycanca, עברית (Hebrew), Tiếng Việt, Українська, Беларуская, Oʻzbekcha, Қазақша, Кыргызча, Türkmençe, Тоҷикӣ, پښتو (Pashto), Kurdî (Kurmancî), မြန်မာ (Burmese), ไทย (Thai), አማርኛ (Amharic) and Bahasa Melayu, 31 languages in all. It opens in your system's language when it is one of them, and you can switch in Settings. Hebrew and Pashto are shown right to left, and Burmese, Thai, Amharic and Hebrew use your system's own fonts for their scripts on Windows, macOS and Linux. Everything is translated, the tray menu and notifications included.

Everything from [2.4.9](https://github.com/umutxyp/Shroudly/releases/tag/v2.4.9) is included. Users of 2.0.4 and later update automatically.
